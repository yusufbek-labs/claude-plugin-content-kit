# Post structures

Pick one that fits the topic.

- **How-to:** problem, why it matters, numbered steps, common mistake, next step.
- **Story and lesson:** short scene, what went wrong or right, the lesson, how the reader can apply it.
- **Myth-busting:** the common belief, why it is wrong or incomplete, what to do instead.
- **Checklist:** one-paragraph promise, grouped checklist, how to start today.
- **Explainer:** plain-language definition, why now, 3 key points, what to do next.

Headline checks: specific, promises one benefit, under about 70 characters, no clickbait.
