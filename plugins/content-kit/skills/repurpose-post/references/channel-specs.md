# Channel specs

These are practical defaults, not platform rules. Confirm current limits before posting.

## LinkedIn
- 120-250 words. Strong first line (it is shown before "see more").
- Short paragraphs with blank lines between; at most one list.
- End with one question or call to action. 0-3 hashtags at the end.

## X
- Single post: aim for 250 characters or fewer. No hashtag stuffing.
- Optional thread: 3-6 posts, each standalone; the first carries the hook, the last carries the call to action.

## Newsletter
- Subject line: under 50 characters. Preview text: under 90 characters.
- Greeting, 2-3 short paragraphs with a personal framing, one clear link line, sign-off in the brand's usual style.
- Do not paste the whole post; tease the main idea and link to it.
